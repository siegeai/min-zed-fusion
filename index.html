Meet your team's minion.

They sit in your meetings, hold what the team knows, and answer when you ask.

Every team gets a minion: a named teammate whose name is its email address, like juno@getmin.ai. You pick a name, invite them to your next team meeting, and they show up. There is no account to create first.

They are in your team meetings. They take the notes, and the notes belong to the team, not to whoever hit record.

Recurring meetings accumulate. A weekly standup becomes one thing that keeps getting deeper, instead of forty unrelated files.

Add them to a doc or a spreadsheet. The same as adding a colleague in Google Docs or Sheets, so they can see the document the meeting is actually about.

Ask them what the team knows, in the app or straight in Slack once they are in your channels.

Your team's context reaches your other tools over MCP, so the same context follows you into whatever AI tool you already use.

Not a notetaker with a login. Other notetakers are built for one person or for a sales team. A minion belongs to the team, so the context is shared by construction, and the name is what makes that legible.
